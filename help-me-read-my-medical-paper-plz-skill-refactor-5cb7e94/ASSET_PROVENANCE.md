The source's published PDF identifies the article as open access under CC BY 4.0. This—not the fact that a manuscript is visible in PMC—is the license basis for these crops. This repository attributes the crops under that license; it does not place them under the repository's MIT license.

The CSHL landing page currently sends its generic “Creative Commons Attribution” link to the older CC BY 3.0 deed, while the published PDF itself explicitly names and links CC BY 4.0. This record follows the license notice printed in the published version. Downstream redistributors should preserve the published-PDF link and recheck the source record if the license metadata changes.
